5. LoRA Adapters (Coming Soon)

    

      Fietsbel supports dynamic loading of LoRA adapters for 
      task-specific fine-tuning. Custom Granite 4 adapters 
      will be published once trained.
    

    
    

      What are LoRAs?
      

        Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) allows efficient fine-tuning 
        of models for specific domains without retraining the 
        entire model. Think of them as "plugins" for AI models.
      

    

    
    

      Deterministic Multi-LoRA Pipeline
      

        Instead of having an AI "choose" which adapter to use, 
        Fietsbel uses structured workflows (n8n/PDL-style) where 
        each step explicitly declares which LoRA to load.
      

      
{
  "pipeline": [
    {"lora": "medical", "task": "extract diagnoses"},
    {"lora": "legal", "task": "identify coverage"},
    {"lora": "financial", "task": "estimate cost"}
  ]
}

7. What Edge AI Can't Do (Yet)

    

      Honesty about limitations builds trust. Here's what 
      small models struggle with:
    

    
    

      Complex Reasoning Failures
      

        
Ask: "If a train leaves at 2pm going 60mph for 3 hours, 
        and another leaves at 3pm going 80mph, when do they meet?"

        
        
        

      

      

        350M will likely fail. 3B might get it right with 
        chain-of-thought prompting.
      

    

    
    

      Context Length Limits
      

        Granite models support up to 8K-32K tokens depending on variant. 
        Very long documents need chunking strategies (RAG).
      

    

    
    

      Hallucinations
      

        
Ask: "When did Napoleon visit America?"

        
        

      

      

        Models will confidently generate false information. 
        Always verify facts against sources.
      

    

    
    

      Creative Writing Quality
      

        Small models (≤3B) produce functional but rarely beautiful prose. 
        For creative work, larger models (7B+) or cloud AI may be better.
